IBA SHINU

Deathrun Prototype — client-only

One very round proto-shiba, one gauntlet of platforms, and ten machines that would rather you did not reach the gate.

  • WASD roll  ·  Space jump, hold in air to tail-glide
  • Shift sprint — burns the Run bar, and roughly doubles your jump
  • Mouse to steer the camera
  • Ten traps along the course arm and fire on their own — the glowing markers tell you which are live
  • R restart run  ·  Esc release mouse
