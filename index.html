IBA SHINU

Deathrun Prototype — client-only

One very round proto-shiba, one gauntlet of platforms, and ten machines that would rather you did not reach the gate.

  • Hold a finger on the screen to run — a stick appears under it — and slide it left or right to steer, or push it up to sprint: faster, and roughly double the jump, while the Run bar lasts. Press with a second finger to jump, and keep it held to tail-glide on the way down. Slide that finger down while it is held and the dog backflips to just behind where it took off: the feint, to bait a trap. The run finger lifted at once is a jump too.
  • WASD roll  ·  Space jump, hold in air to tail-glide
  • Shift sprint — burns the Run bar, and roughly doubles your jump
  • F mid-jump — flip back to just behind where you took off, the feint that baits a trap
  • Mouse to steer the camera
  • Ten traps along the course arm and fire on their own — the glowing markers tell you which are live
  • As the enemy you are behind the glass instead: the pack is all bots and the traps fire for nobody but you. A gallery runs the whole length of the course, and every machine has one button, on the wall level with it. You have to walk to a button to use it — stand at it and press E. The course is hundreds of metres long and they cover it at speed, so you cannot hold all of it. Stop all six getting through.
  • R restart run  ·  Esc release mouse
